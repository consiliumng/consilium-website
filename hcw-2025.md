The Consilium Nigerian Health Worker Report 2025 is the first primary research study of its scale on the Nigerian health workforce. Drawing on survey responses from 537 healthcare workers across 35 states and the FCT, the study documents compensation structures, working conditions, burnout prevalence, and the factors shaping emigration intent.
