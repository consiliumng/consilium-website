How much has healthcare cost the average Nigerian risen since 2011? This report benchmarks official health prices, medicine and test prices, and the minimum wage on each election year from 2011 to 2026.

Official health prices are now about 6.9 times their 2011 level, while prices overall are about 9.2 times. Item by item, prices rose between 1.8 and 9.4 times, and a malaria course now takes about 3.6 days of minimum-wage pay. Every figure links to its source, and the page states the limits of the data plainly.
